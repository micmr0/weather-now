FAQ

Everything you need to know

Is Weather Now free?

Yes. Weather Now FREE includes the essential weather information you need every day, including current conditions, hourly forecasts, air quality and activity planning.

What is included in Weather Now PLUS?

PLUS removes ads and unlocks weather maps, hourly precipitation and wind charts, plus up to 2 saved locations.

What is included in Weather Now PRO?

PRO includes everything in PLUS, adds the Home Screen Weather Widget and increases the saved-location limit to 5.

Does Weather Now work worldwide?

Yes. You can search for weather in cities and locations around the world and quickly return to your current position whenever you need it.

Can I restore my subscription?

Yes. Sign in with the same Google account used for the purchase and Weather Now will check your active Google Play subscription.

Does the app work offline?

Previously loaded weather data remains available from the local cache when the network is temporarily unavailable.
